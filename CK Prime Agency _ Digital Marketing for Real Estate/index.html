Questions, answered

What do you need from me to get started?

Tell us about your business and your goals. Share any photos, videos or logos you already have and we'll take it from there.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the work. A social post or short video is quicker than a full website. After a short conversation we'll give you a clear timeline.

Do you only work with real estate agencies?

Real estate is our focus, but we also work with other businesses that want a premium digital presence.

Can you manage my social media every month?

Yes. We plan, create, post and reply for you, so your pages stay active and on brand.

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?

Ad spend is paid to the advertising platform. We plan and run the campaigns, and we agree the budget with you first.
